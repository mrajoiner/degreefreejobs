Your account and resume.

Sign-in is provided by ChatGPT. We use your verified identity and email to create your Degree Free Jobs account when you confirm your email and save. Your profile can contain your name, phone, street address, city, state, and ZIP code. These details are suggested from resume text on your device; you can review and correct them before saving.

When you choose to save, your profile and resume text are stored in the site's database and your original file is stored privately on the hosting platform. Access is restricted on the server to your signed-in account and authorized administrators. Administrators can manage accounts and download saved resumes; account changes and resume access by administrators are logged. We do not send your resume to employers or AI services.

Your account and resume remain until you remove them. Account deletion removes your profile, resume text, and original file from active storage. Limited audit records retain account identifiers and actions for security; hosting logs, backup retention, and international-transfer arrangements require operator review. No claim of full GDPR compliance is made.

Saved jobs and application tracking remain in this browser. Resume text and files are processed in browser memory before saving, and are no longer retained in browser storage. Previously saved browser resume data is removed when you visit the updated main page.

No outside font calls. No added ad trackers.

Fonts, map files, and resume-reading libraries are served by this site. Our application does not include advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or marketing cookies. Browser storage supports the resume, save, and tracking features you choose to use.

Opening the site still uses hosting.

The hosting provider processes technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the site. Browser-only resume storage does not eliminate that hosting processing. Provider retention and international-transfer arrangements require separate operator review.

You choose when to leave.

Job feeds are retrieved by our server. Employer links open a separate website with its own privacy policy and application form. Your resume is not sent just by clicking a job link. We send no referrer header with links from this application.

The button below removes old local resume data, saved jobs, and application notes from this browser. Use My account to remove your server-stored resume or account. Applications submitted directly to employers are managed by those employers.
